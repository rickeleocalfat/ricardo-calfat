The Story

A game of
mistaken identity

When the girls’ soccer team gets cut, Viola refuses to sit on the bench of her own life. Her twin brother is skipping town for two weeks, so she does the only logical thing: she cuts her hair, borrows his name, and shows up at his new school ready to make the boys’ team.

The problem? Being a boy is harder than it looks. Between locker-room small talk, a roommate she definitely did not plan on liking, and a rival team gunning for the season title, Viola has to keep her secret alive long enough to prove she belongs on the field.

It’s a fast, funny story about who we pretend to be, who we really are, and how the truth has a way of scoring in the last minute. Cheer loud — somebody’s about to get caught.
